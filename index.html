How to play

You get seven prompts — anime and video games — starting easy and getting harder each round. Type any valid answer within 30 seconds.

Every right answer becomes an attack. Common answers land small hits; rare ones unleash bigger forms. A Mythic answer is an instant K.O. Common and Uncommon hits get countered: the rival strikes back for 20 HP.

Three answers we don't recognise, or the timer running out, and the rival counterattacks. Damage carries over between rounds — knock a rival out and the next one steps in: the Oni Brute, then the Iron Warden, then the Hollow, and round again. Lose all your HP and the run ends.

Your score is the total damage you deal. It sets your rank: Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum.
